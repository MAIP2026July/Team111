# Wayfinder AI

**AI-Powered Content Generation and Knowledge Assistance for Frontline Learning**

## 📌 About the Project

Wayfinder AI is a hypothetical AI product concept developed for the **MBA ZG583 – Management of AI Products** course.

The product aims to help frontline and operational workforces by keeping training content up to date and providing workers with quick, trustworthy, source-cited answers through an AI-powered knowledge assistant.

For the complete product strategy, architecture, roadmap, risks, and success metrics, please refer to the project document included in this repository.
