## Module overview
Daily forklift inspection guide for warehouse operators.

## Checklist
1. Battery charge at least 30 percent.
2. Forks free of cracks, bends and uneven wear.
3. Tyres free of damage and embedded objects.
4. Horn, lights, reversing beeper and seat belt working.
5. Service brake and parking brake working.
6. No hydraulic leaks under the mast or around cylinders.
7. Record on the checklist and tag Out of Service if any item fails.
