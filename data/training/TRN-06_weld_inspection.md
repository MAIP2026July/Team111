## Module overview
Inspection of welded brackets in Welding Cell 2.

## Steps
1. Inspect 5 brackets from every 100 produced.
2. Check welds for porosity, cracks and undercut.
3. Check the mounting gap in fixture WB-7 is within 0.5 mm.
4. Minimum weld leg length is 6 mm.
5. Stop the cell and call quality if 2 or more brackets in a sample fail.
