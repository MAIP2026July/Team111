## Module overview
Safe and accurate use of click-type torque wrenches.

## Key points
1. Torque wrenches are calibrated every 180 days; check the sticker before use.
2. Wind the wrench down to its lowest setting after use.
3. Never use a torque wrench to loosen fasteners.
