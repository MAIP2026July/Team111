## Module overview
This module teaches maintenance technicians how to service the conveyor C3 side guards.

## Steps
1. Lock out the conveyor before removing any guard.
2. Remove the guards and check for cracks and missing bolts.
3. Tighten the guard bolts to 25 Nm using a torque wrench.
4. Check the gap between the guard and the belt edge is no more than 10 mm.
5. Run the conveyor empty to check for rubbing.
