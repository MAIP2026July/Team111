## Module overview
Introduction to die changeover on press HP-2 for new stamping operators.

## Steps
1. Stop the press at top dead centre and lock it out.
2. Install the safety block.
3. Unclamp and roll out the old die.
4. Roll in the new die and clamp it.
5. Set the clamp pressure to 160 bar.
6. Run 3 slow single strokes and send the first part to quality.
