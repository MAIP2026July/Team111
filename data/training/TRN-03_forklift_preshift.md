## Module overview
How to complete the forklift pre-shift inspection.

## Checklist
1. Battery charge at least 30 percent.
2. Forks free of cracks, bends and uneven wear.
3. Tyres free of damage and embedded objects.
4. Horn, lights, reversing beeper and seat belt working.
5. Service brake and parking brake working on a level surface.
6. No hydraulic leaks under the mast.
7. Record on the pre-shift checklist; tag Out of Service if any item fails.
