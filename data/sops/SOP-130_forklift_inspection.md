## 1. Purpose
Ensure forklifts are safe to operate before each shift.

## 2. Procedure
1. Check the battery charge indicator shows at least 30 percent before use.
2. Inspect forks for cracks, bends and uneven wear.
3. Check tyres for damage and embedded objects.
4. Test the horn, lights, reversing beeper and seat belt.
5. Test service brake and parking brake on a level surface.
6. Check for hydraulic leaks under the mast and around the cylinders.
7. Record the inspection on the pre-shift checklist. If any item fails, tag the forklift Out of Service and inform your supervisor.
