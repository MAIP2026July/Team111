## 1. Purpose
Change dies on press HP-2 safely and bring the press back to production quality.

## 2. Procedure
1. Complete the last good part check and stop the press at top dead centre.
2. Apply lockout/tagout per SOP-101 and install the safety block between the platens.
3. Unclamp the old die and roll it out onto the die cart using the roller bolsters.
4. Clean the bolster plate and inspect the clamp T-slots for debris.
5. Roll in the new die, centre it using the locating pins, and clamp with all four hydraulic clamps.
6. Set the clamp pressure to 180 bar and confirm on the clamp gauge.
7. Remove the safety block and locks, then run 3 single strokes at slow speed.
8. Submit the first part for first-article inspection before starting the production run.
