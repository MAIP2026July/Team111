## 1. Purpose
Contain chemical spills safely and protect people and the environment.

## 2. Procedure
1. Stop work and warn people nearby. Keep everyone upwind of the spill.
2. If the spill is larger than 20 litres, unknown, or causing symptoms, evacuate the area and call extension 2222.
3. For small known spills, check the Safety Data Sheet (SDS) for the correct PPE before approaching.
4. Wear nitrile gloves, splash goggles and an apron as a minimum.
5. Contain the spill using the yellow spill kit; work from the outside edge toward the centre.
6. Place used absorbent in a labelled hazardous waste bag and report the spill to your supervisor.
