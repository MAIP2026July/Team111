## 1. Purpose
Detect weld defects on mounting brackets before they leave the cell.

## 2. Sampling
Inspect 5 brackets from every 100 produced, and the first bracket after any fixture change.

## 3. Procedure
1. Visually check every weld for porosity, cracks and undercut.
2. Place the bracket in gauge fixture WB-7 and check the mounting gap is within 0.5 mm.
3. Measure weld leg length with a fillet gauge; minimum leg length is 6 mm.
4. Mark passed brackets with a green dot. Place failed brackets in the red quarantine bin.
5. If 2 or more brackets in one sample fail, stop the cell and call the quality technician.
