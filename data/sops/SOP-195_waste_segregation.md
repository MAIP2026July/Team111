## 1. Purpose
Separate waste correctly so it can be recycled or disposed of safely.

## 2. Bins
1. Blue bin: clean cardboard and paper.
2. Green bin: clean plastic film and stretch wrap.
3. Grey bin: scrap metal and swarf.
4. Red bin: oily rags, used absorbent and other hazardous waste.
5. Black bin: general non-recyclable waste only.
