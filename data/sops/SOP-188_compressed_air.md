## 1. Purpose
Keep plant compressed air at the right pressure and free of water.

## 2. Procedure
1. Check the main header pressure gauge reads between 6 and 7 bar; the target is 6.5 bar.
2. Drain the receiver tank condensate valve until only air comes out.
3. Check the dryer dew point display shows 3 degrees C or lower.
4. Listen for air leaks at drops and quick couplers; tag any leak found with a yellow leak tag.
5. Record readings on the utilities daily log.
