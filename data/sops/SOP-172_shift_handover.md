## 1. Purpose
Make sure the incoming shift knows the status of equipment, quality and safety issues.

## 2. Procedure
1. Hold a 10 minute face-to-face handover at the line board at the start of each shift.
2. Review open safety issues, equipment out of service and quality holds.
3. Update the shift log with production count, downtime and any open work orders.
4. The incoming lead signs the shift log to accept the handover.
