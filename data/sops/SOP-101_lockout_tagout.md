## 1. Purpose
Prevent injury from unexpected start-up or release of stored energy during service or maintenance.

## 2. When Required
Lockout/tagout is required before any person places any part of their body inside a guard, point of operation, or danger zone of powered equipment.

## 3. Procedure
1. Notify all affected operators that the equipment will be shut down and locked out.
2. Shut down the equipment using normal stopping controls.
3. Isolate all energy sources: electrical, pneumatic, hydraulic, gravity and stored spring energy.
4. Apply your personal lock and a signed danger tag to each isolation point. One person, one lock, one key.
5. Release or restrain stored energy: bleed air and hydraulic lines, block raised parts.
6. Verify zero energy by attempting a normal start, then return controls to OFF.
7. Only the person who applied a lock may remove it, after confirming all tools are removed and all people are clear.
