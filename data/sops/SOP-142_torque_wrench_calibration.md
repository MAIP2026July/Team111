## 1. Purpose
Keep torque tools accurate so fasteners are tightened to specification.

## 2. Calibration
1. Every torque wrench must be calibrated every 90 days.
2. Check the calibration sticker before use. Do not use a wrench past its due date; return it to the tool crib.

## 3. Handling
1. After use, wind a click-type wrench down to its lowest setting before storage.
2. Never use a torque wrench to loosen fasteners.
3. Report any wrench that has been dropped; it must be re-calibrated before use.
