## 1. Purpose
Keep conveyor C3 side guards secure to prevent contact with moving belts and rollers.

## 2. Frequency
Inspect guards at the start of every shift. Perform full guard maintenance every 2 weeks.

## 3. Tools and Materials
Calibrated torque wrench (10-50 Nm), 13 mm socket, thread-locking compound (blue), replacement M8 bolts.

## 4. Procedure
1. Apply lockout/tagout per SOP-101 before removing any guard.
2. Remove the side guard panels and inspect for cracks, dents and missing fasteners.
3. Replace any damaged M8 guard bolts; do not reuse bolts with stripped threads.
4. Apply blue thread-locking compound to each guard bolt.
5. Tighten the guard bolts to 30 Nm using a calibrated torque wrench.
6. Check the gap between the guard and the belt edge is no more than 8 mm.
7. Remove locks per SOP-101 and run the conveyor empty for 2 minutes to check for rubbing or noise.

## 5. Records
Record the maintenance in the CMMS work order, including bolt replacements.
